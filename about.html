The story so far

I started far from design: selling ad space to local businesses in Goa, onboarding retailers onto a SaaS product, running campaigns across 14 brands for a gaming and hospitality group. Every one of those jobs was about getting people to trust a promise and then keeping it.

At QYou, I built a TV network's content library from the ground up, licensing from 300+ creators and partners, and helped keep a 24×7 broadcast schedule on air for 650M+ viewers. Live broadcast doesn't forgive missed handoffs. I learned that nothing ships on time without a system behind it.

In 2022 I joined Lollypop Design Studio as a project manager. 170+ product initiatives later, I lead its program function: 11 PMs, 250+ designers, 45+ engineers and 35 programs running at once across 7 centres.

The scale changed. The job didn't: build the conditions where good people do their best work, then get out of their way.
